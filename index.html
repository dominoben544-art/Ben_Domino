BEN DOMINO

Ben Domino is a domino artist from the Philippines specializing in large-scale and visually impressive domino projects. He demonstrates precision and creativity by transforming simple pieces into remarkable setups.

ABOUT ME

I am Ben Domino, a 16-year-old domino artist from the Philippines, formerly known as “Golbergmino.” I create huge and phenomenal domino projects using over 3,000 wooden domino pieces, combining patience, precision, and creativity in every build. My passion for domino art drives me to design intricate setups and push my limits to create more complex and visually impressive displays. Through my work, I aim to inspire others and showcase how simple dominoes can be transformed into something extraordinary. I continuously explore new techniques and creative concepts to improve my craft and make each project more unique than the last. With dedication and perseverance, I strive to grow as an artist and share my creations with a wider audience.
